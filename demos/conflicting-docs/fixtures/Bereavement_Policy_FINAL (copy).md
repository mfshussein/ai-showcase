# Marsa Airways Bereavement Fare Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective: 2022-03-01
Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets   out how Marsa Airways supports customers who must travel because of the death of an immediate family member.

## 2 Eligibility
Immediate family means spouse, parent, child, sibling, grandparent or grandchild. The customer must be the person travelling.

## 3 Fares
A discount of up to thirty percent applies to the lowest available economy fare on the day of booking. The discount cannot be combined with promotional fares.

## 4 Claims
### 4.1 Documentation
A death certificate, funeral notice or letter from the hospital must be provided within 14 days of booking.
### 4.2 Retroactive claims
A customer who travels before applying may submit a request for a bereavement refund within 90 days of the ticket issue date, and the difference between the fare paid and the bereavement fare will be refunded to the original form of payment.

## 5 Contact
Requests are handled by Customer Relations at care@marsa.example or through the Marsa app.
