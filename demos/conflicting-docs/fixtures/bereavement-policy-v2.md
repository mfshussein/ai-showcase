# Marsa Airways Bereavement Fare Policy
Version: 2.0
Effective: 2024-06-01
Owner: Customer Relations

## 1 Purpose
This policy sets out how Marsa Airways supports customers who must travel because of the death of an immediate family member.

## 2 Eligibility
Immediate family means spouse, parent, child, sibling, grandparent or grandchild. The customer must be the person travelling.

## 3 Fare
A discount of up to 30% applies to the lowest available economy fare on the day of booking. The discount cannot be combined with promotional fares.

## 4 Claims
### 4.1 Documentation
A death certificate, funeral notice or letter from the hospital must be provided within 30 days of booking.
### 4.2 Retroactive claims
Bereavement fares must be requested before travel. Refund requests submitted after travel has taken place will not be approved. Customers who are unable to apply before travel should contact Customer Relations before departure so that a note can be placed on the booking.

## 5 Contact
Requests are handled by Customer Relations at care@marsa.example or through the Marsa app.
