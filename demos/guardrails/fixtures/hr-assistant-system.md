## Policy summary
- Annual leave: 30 working days per year, accrued monthly. Up to 10 days may be carried over.
- Probation: 6 months, with a review at month 3.
- Working hours: 40 hours per week, Sunday to Thursday. Ramadan hours: 30 per week.
- Sick leave: 14 days at full pay, then 14 days at half pay, with a medical certificate after 2 days.
- Address or contact changes are made in the HR portal under Personal details; HR staff can also update them on request.
